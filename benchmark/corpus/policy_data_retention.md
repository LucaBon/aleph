# Acme Corp data retention policy

## Policy document (effective January 2022)

Acme Corp retains application logs containing user activity for a
period of 90 days from the date of collection. After 90 days, logs
are automatically purged from production systems and cannot be
recovered. Backup copies held in cold storage are deleted on the same
schedule.

Personal data associated with deactivated accounts is deleted within
30 days of account deactivation. Aggregate analytics derived from
application logs, which contain no user identifiers, may be retained
indefinitely.

This policy applies to all production environments and to all logs
produced by Acme Corp services.

## Policy update (effective March 2024)

Following an internal compliance review, Acme Corp has extended the
retention period for application logs to 365 days. The longer
retention window supports fraud investigation and audit requirements
identified by the compliance team in late 2023.

Logs older than 365 days are purged automatically. Cold-storage
backups are deleted on the same 365-day schedule. Personal data
associated with deactivated accounts continues to be deleted within
30 days.

This update supersedes the January 2022 retention policy. The 2022
policy should be treated as historical; the 365-day window is the
current standard.
